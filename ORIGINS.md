# Origin starting packages

These are original house rules for this D&D × MOTW xianxia game, created with the project owner's authorization. Genre sources inform the kinds of possessions and differences between backgrounds; **the amounts below are design choices, not a universal xianxia economy**.

## Money

| Origin | Low-grade spirit stones | Silver taels | Package emphasis |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Sect Disciple | 12 | 5 | Standard issue, provisions, sect token |
| Rogue Cultivator | 6 | 3 | Self-sufficient travel and gathering |
| Clan Heir | 30 | 15 | Family purse, formal belongings, extra supplies |
| Wandering Doctor | 8 | 8 | Medical tools and ordinary remedies |
| Spirit Beast in Human Form | 3 | 1 | Gathered goods and a small acquired purse |
| Demonic Cultivator | 8 | 3 | Portable ritual materials and concealment |
| Mortal Scholar | 0 | 12 | Ordinary silver, books, writing tools |
| Fallen Young Master | 4 | 6 | Remaining possessions after loss of wealth |

Spirit stones and silver are tracked separately. No conversion rate, shop prices, recurring stipend, resale values or automatic resource regeneration are established here. A richer background is intentionally richer; these packages are not equal-value point-buy builds. All give zero attribute modifiers.

## Shared travel basics

Every package includes one set of plain travel clothes, one weather cloak, one cloth shoulder pack, one waterskin, one bedroll, and one flint-and-tinder kit. The pack is ordinary, not magical storage. Each ration supplies one day's food; water still needs refilling.

## Additional supplies by origin

- **Sect Disciple:** 7 daily rations, 1 training sword, 1 sect identity token, 2 doses of basic healing salve, 1 signal talisman, 1 sect handbook.
- **Rogue Cultivator:** 10 daily rations, 1 utility knife, 10 metres of hemp rope, 1 herb-gathering sickle, 3 empty medicine vials, 1 dose of basic healing salve, 1 local trail map.
- **Clan Heir:** 7 daily rations, 1 fine steel sword, 1 set of spare formal robes, 1 clan seal, 3 doses of basic healing salve, 2 signal talismans, 1 family keepsake.
- **Wandering Doctor:** 7 daily rations, 1 utility knife, 1 physician tool roll (needles, tweezers, small scissors, mortar), 6 clean bandage rolls, 6 doses of basic healing salve, 4 dried medicinal herb packets, 4 empty medicine vials, 1 herbal reference notebook.
- **Spirit Beast in Human Form:** 5 daily rations, 1 walking staff, 3 foraged herb packets, 3 small polished stones, 1 keepsake from a former dwelling. These trade goods are ordinary materials; the polished stones are not additional spirit stones.
- **Demonic Cultivator:** 7 daily rations, 1 ordinary ritual dagger, 1 plain face covering, 10 incense sticks, 10 sheets of blank talisman paper, 1 ink-and-brush set, 2 sealed pouches of ordinary ritual materials (salt, ash, charcoal), 2 doses of basic healing salve.
- **Mortal Scholar:** 7 daily rations, 1 walking staff, 1 ink-and-brush set, 20 sheets of paper, 2 bound notebooks, 1 classical text, 1 regional map, 1 oil lantern, 2 flasks of lamp oil.
- **Fallen Young Master:** 5 daily rations, 1 serviceable worn steel sword, 1 set of mended formal robes, 1 tarnished family signet, 1 dose of basic healing salve, 1 old family letter.

## Scope of item effects

Basic salve supports treatment of ordinary cuts; numerical healing is not defined. Signal talismans produce a visible distress flare without guaranteeing rescue. Blank talisman paper is unenchanted. Books and toolkits do not grant techniques or skill bonuses. Weapon damage, medical move effects, carry limits and future shopping rules belong to later design steps. Appearance, status objects and ritual materials grant no hidden powers, guaranteed authority or access.

Origin describes a background. Selecting Demonic Cultivator here does not force Demonic Path, and selecting Spirit Beast in Human Form does not change the path dropdown. Special Heritage remains available only under the Beast / Plant Spirit Path, as instructed.

## Source basis

- [Wuxiaworld's general glossary](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/page/general-glossary-of-terms) describes spirit stones as cultivator currency, sect support and resource hierarchies, independent rogue cultivators, clan inheritance, medicines and talismans. These support the package themes; they do not determine any quantity above.
- [An Alchemist's Path to Eternity, chapter 3](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/alchemists-path-to-eternity/apte-chapter-3) depicts a market for talismans and consumables and the material pressures of independent cultivation. Its prices are specific to that novel and are not imported here.
- [Talisman Emperor, chapter 5](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/talisman-emperor/te-book-1-chapter-5) illustrates scarce spirit stones competing between cultivation and buying instructional material. That supports keeping modest starting purses and avoiding automatic rare treasures.

## Saved character behavior

Selecting an origin replaces its starting allocation rather than adding another copy. Money and supplies appear in Identity, the gear step and the printable review sheet. The origin package is stored as a versioned snapshot in character saves. Existing saves acquire the matching package on load; old custom origins are preserved without invented allocations. Manual equipment notes remain separate and unchanged. This is a character-creation allocation, not a live inventory or spending tracker.

## Special Heritage catalogue

The original beast-wheel answer in [Xianxia Worldbuilding Setup](https://chatgpt.com/c/6a9b3b58-0160-83e9-88d7-bfd32ef9276d) supplies all **550 unique names**. Source turn: `dc7f0e23-fa60-4a3e-8b12-750613b79a3e` (request beginning “give me a long long list of beasts to add to a wheel”).

The dropdowns use these browsing categories: Mammal, Bird, Reptile, Amphibian, Fish, Marine Beast, Mollusk, Crustacean, Insect, Arachnid, Myriapod, Other Invertebrate, Deep-Sea / Unusual Heritage, and Mythic Beast. Categories preserve the source's informal groupings, including its unusual placements; they are not a claim about biological taxonomy. The source's zodiac and folkloric subsets overlap the main catalogue, so exact repeated names appear once. Dog, unique to the zodiac subset, is included under Mammal. Distinct source spellings and aliases remain selectable.

The wheel contains no plant catalogue. Plant Spirit (custom) therefore accepts a typed plant name, and Custom heritage preserves earlier free-text choices. Choosing a new category clears the previous refined choice to prevent mismatches. Switching away from Beast / Plant Spirit Path hides both choices without deleting them. Heritage grants no automatic stats, elemental affinity, cultivation realm or divine powers at this stage.
