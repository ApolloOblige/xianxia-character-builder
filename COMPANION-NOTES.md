# Vault-Devouring Rat

Primary source: the creator’s September 12, 2026 description and supplied portrait: a shadow-bodied rat, handheld when young, growing with cultivation rather than age, infused from its master’s roots, specializing in stealth and speed. The existing Silent Vault-Rat Scripture provides artifact-devouring and bonded-companion context.

Reference: [D&D Basic Rules — Using Ability Scores](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/using-ability-scores), for agility/stealth and perception as distinct capabilities. All numerical companion rules here are original house rules for the builder’s five-attribute, 2d6 system, not D&D statistics.

Growth bands: Mortal/Body Tempering handheld; Qi Refining/Foundation cat-sized; Golden Core/Nascent Soul hound-sized; Spirit Transformation through Heavenly Gate panther-sized; immortal realms 2–3 m. Age and master realm do not enter the calculation. Stage does not add a separate companion increase.

Vitality = 6 + beast Body + twice beast realm rank. Qi = 3 + beast Flow + realm rank. The beast has its own stats; none are added to the master. Infusion offers one conditional trait from a currently selected master root, carries its Yin/Yang manifestation, and grants no copied root purity or permanent attribute increase. Removed roots make saved infusions inactive. All situational bonuses share the existing +2 cap.

The beast’s personal actions use its own Qi. Existing Scripture arts retain their specified rolls and master-paid costs. Complex commanded attacks consume the master’s action to avoid granting an unrestricted extra combat turn. Advancement, training and injuries remain part of play; the builder records choices and reference values rather than tracking current resource spending.

The portrait is the user-supplied image, copied without alterations.
