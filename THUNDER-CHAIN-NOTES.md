# Shadow-Splitting Thunder Chain Art

Source: the creator’s pasted expanded manual, supplied September 12, 2026. The 18 names, five chapter groupings, weapon, elemental roles and Yin tradeoffs follow that text. Numeric costs, ranges, action timings, resolution limits and mastery prerequisites are original adaptations for this builder, not official D&D rules.

Yin is an optional enhancement attached to an established Yin source, not an independent elemental root. Each use adds exactly 1 Qi. Combined arts pay that increase once; their listed cost includes their component effects. Shadow Bind instead enhances an existing physical hold and explicitly lists the separate setup cost.

Movement progresses from Thunderstep (1 Qi, action) through anchored Dark Current Reversal (2, reaction) and concealed Black Lightning Withdrawal (3, post-strike reaction). Afterimage (3) is a brief visual deception; Empty Shadow Escape (5) has a longer visual/spiritual decoy and longer movement. Needle Fang (2) accelerates a normal strike; Tanto Through the Gap (3) exploits a previously observed armor gap. One Breath (6) combines entry, one strike and exit; Stormless Kill (7) requires vulnerable preparation and adds disruption rather than automatic death.

Mastery is a player-recorded training milestone agreed with the GM. Advanced arts require all leading arts learned, mastered, and currently available. Saved choices remain when a manual, chapter or prerequisite becomes inactive, but those arts and their descendants are excluded from the reference sheet. Old saves begin with no recorded mastery. Only this manual receives a new prerequisite tree; the prior rat manual remains intact.

The matching Thunder Chain Cultivator discipline grants the specified kusarigama with tanto-ended chain as reference equipment. No permanent attribute bonuses are added by learning or mastering techniques.
