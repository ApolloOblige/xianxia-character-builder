# Golden Furnace and the two manuals

Primary source: [creator’s supplied Google document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lOpPjhd13loAc9RHOp9UDNQIF26_DH0Vb6R9lwp8uiM/edit), read September 12, 2026.

The Golden Furnace Physique retains a hot, toxin-metabolizing body and controlled internal poison refinement. Its conditional +1 follows the existing physique system; it grants neither a new root nor automatic immunity. No unsupported permanent resource increase was added.

Ten Thousand Venoms Cauldron-Puppet Scripture preserves all 18 arts and its five chapter groupings (4/5/5/2/2). The puppet operates through engraved routines and commands until the final two awakening arts. Learning an art is not proof of successful awakening; the final result depends on events in play, and an awakened alchemist has independent judgment. Yang-Venom Ignition specifically requires the source’s 100%-pure Yang Poison root; the rest of the manual remains available without that personal specialization.

Ninefold Crushing Force Manual preserves all ten arts and uses Body, a heavy hammer or comparable weapon, stance and momentum. It requires no named weapon, element or physique. The explicit mastery chains are Ironroot Stance → Whole-Frame Strike → Mountain-Breaking Blow; Returning Momentum → Endless Pendulum; Sky-Lifting Strike → Falling Mountain. Three study chapters organize the ten arts; those chapter titles are new organizational additions because the source supplied none.

Qi costs, timings, ranges, roll outcomes, additional prerequisite links and quantities are original tabletop expansions. Source recipes and ingredients still determine poison effects; the catalogue does not grant arbitrary poisons or free supplies. Fuel sustains ordinary puppet operation while listed technique costs use master Qi. No physical poison-making recipes are provided. Combat harm additions refer to the weapon’s existing harm value.

The source contains conversational drafting remarks. Those remarks are omitted from the player-facing catalogue; the established lore and technique content are retained.
