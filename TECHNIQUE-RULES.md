# Silent Vault-Rat Scripture techniques

## Source and scope

The owner's [custom manual](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nzSOxOvV_ETWHizq6jotzGBqaMNrM52bXlYPmJhkYz0/edit) supplies the Silent Vault-Rat Scripture, its 18 named arts, four chapters, companion dependence and narrative limitations. Read September 12, 2026. Only this manual has received selectable techniques. Other manuals have not been expanded.

The document contains no numerical Qi costs, fixed distances, durations, storage capacity, roll outcomes, rest rules or realm thresholds. Those are original tabletop adaptations, supplied under the owner's authorization to fill mechanical gaps. Chapter access is set by study rather than imposing an unsupported realm ladder. No art is learned merely by selecting the manual.

Each card contains the effect, timing, limits and resolution. 0-Qi arts are repeatable but retain time/material requirements. Companion effects spend the tamer's Qi, not an invented second resource pool. Qi is the existing fungible reserve, with free arts serving the cantrip role; no D&D leveled-slot table has been added. Selecting an art does not spend Qi, and this character-creation page does not track live expenditures.

Quiet Devouring is a 1-Qi supplement to a learned Seal-Gnawing Fang (3 total) or Gnawed Meridian Sabotage (4 total). It cannot be used alone. One Shadow, Two Lives costs 5 Qi and refreshes only on a full cultivation rest. Devouring Tooth Refinement consumes material but grants no immediate stat increase, copied power or Qi refund. Broken-Treasure Assimilation supplies one temporary situational benefit and never lets the tamer digest artifacts.

Existing technique, training and cultivation benefits share the +2 positive situational cap before drawbacks. Noncombat effects need no roll unless uncertain or contested. Failed attempts still cost Qi. An action occupies the main turn, reactions are immediate responses limited to one per round, and a scene is one continuous encounter. Companion capability and fictional positioning remain necessary; the manual does not conjure an Artifact-Devouring Rat.

## Cultivation recovery

A brief cultivation rest lasts one uninterrupted safe hour and restores half maximum Qi rounded up, capped at maximum; it benefits the character once between full rests. A full rest takes eight hours and restores all Qi and once-per-full-rest uses, at most once per 24 hours. Both require a suitable qi source. Interruption prevents recovery. Rest does not automatically heal, advance realms or grant mastery.

[D&D's Rules Glossary](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary) provides the one-hour/eight-hour downtime reference; [D&D's spell rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spells) provide the contrast between repeatable cantrips and limited-use magic. The Qi amounts and cultivation restrictions here are house rules, not D&D rules.

## Interface and saves

Techniques is page four, after Disciplines & Gear and before Attributes. Select Silent Vault-Rat Scripture in either manual slot, choose the studied chapter, and mark learned arts. Only learned arts from the currently selected manual and available chapter appear on the review sheet. Removing the manual or lowering its chapter retains selections but makes affected arts inactive until restored. Existing six-page saves migrate their Attributes, Story and Review positions to the matching new pages, once. The original save key is retained.
