# Cultivation rules and references

## Established world structure

Recovered from [Xianxia Worldbuilding Setup](https://chatgpt.com/c/6a9b3b58-0160-83e9-88d7-bfd32ef9276d), particularly the user's edited realm list (69c1c6d2-52bb-4180-b5dc-34507fc15df2), the continuation (345f1355-6914-44eb-8a35-7ae6089fc7e7), and the user's root reference (0cbf6de4-941c-4866-b20d-b2042ee43ef4).

The realm sequence is Mortal, Body Tempering (5 stages), Qi Refining (7), Foundation Establishment (7), Golden Core (5), Nascent Soul (5), Spirit Transformation (4), Dao Integration (3), Heavenly Gate (3), Earth Immortal (3), Spirit Immortal (3), Celestial Immortal (3), True Immortal (3). From Dao Integration onward the stages are Early, Middle, Late. Ascension is a transition, and divine authority is a separate jurisdiction rather than an extra cultivation realm. Lifespan ranges and final-stage breakthroughs follow that chat. Body Tempering uses the owner's corrected five stages.

The five ordinary roots, six mutated roots, Yin/Yang polar roots and their resonance/suppression relationships follow the user's supplied reference. All 15 common physiques are retained; named rare and heavenly examples include the character's Taiyin Body. The broader source discussions are inspiration, not universal rules shared by every xianxia novel.

## Numerical house rules added with authorization

Realm rank is 0 for Mortal through 12 for True Immortal. The rank bonus is 2 × rank Vitality and max(0, 3 × (rank − 1)) Qi. Within numbered realms, add floor((stage − 1) / 2) to both resources. In three-stage realms, Early/Middle/Late add 0/2/4. Total Vitality = 6 + total Body + realm/stage + physique + optional adjustment. Total Qi = 3 + total Flow + realm/stage + physique + optional adjustment. These are playable resource tracks, not a literal measure of immortal power. Realm supremacy also governs fictional permission and scale. No automatic core-attribute increase is granted by cultivation.

Choose up to five distinct roots. Each has independent purity from 1–100%; percentages need not sum to 100. New roots begin at editable 50%. Missing purity stays unknown. Purity below 20 gives no affinity roll bonus; 20 or higher gives +1 for that root's specified activity. Apply at most one root bonus per roll. Cultivation progress for the corresponding element is multiplied by 0.5 (1–19), 0.75 (20–39), 1 (40–59), 1.25 (60–79), or 1.5 (80–100). For a method requiring multiple elements use the lowest multiplier. This is a reference for awarded cultivation progress, not an automatic XP clock or a combat multiplier.

An actively supplied resonant element grants +1; active suppression imposes −1. At most one interaction adjustment applies per roll, with both cancelling. Merely possessing two roots does not automatically activate resonance. Opposing roots can coexist. An overwhelming realm gap takes precedence over elemental matchups.

Each physique gives listed resource increases and +1 for its stated activity, plus any explicit drawback. Combined positive root, resonance and physique roll adjustments are capped at +2 before drawbacks. This cap does not alter the saved core-attribute total or unrelated manual modifiers. Physiques do not grant missing roots. All numerical physique effects are original adaptations; named constitutions do not import unlimited novel powers.

## Compatibility

Existing free-text realm, stage, roots and purity remain stored and visible. Older root descriptions remain mechanically active only until structured root selections are made; legacy Fire Root then stops adding a duplicate effect. Unknown custom realms require an optional resource adjustment to calculate a resource total. Changing realm clears the old stage. Changing physique replaces its resource effect. New automatic effects are recomputed, never appended to the manual modifier list. Existing additional resource values are retained as adjustments.

## External reference basis

- [Wuxiaworld glossary](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/page/general-glossary-of-terms): genre terminology for qi, meridians, dantian, Golden Core, Nascent Soul, Five Phases and immortal cultivation. It supplies context, not these numerical values.
- [Monster of the Week playbooks — Evil Hat](https://evilhat.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Monster-of-the-Week-Hunter-Playbooks-Consolidated-2025.pdf): small ratings and +1 improvements inform the restrained roll-bonus scale.
- [D&D Free Rules: Creating a Character](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/creating-a-character): structured character choices inform the creation flow; D&D ability scores and leveling tables are not transplanted into the 2d6 system.

Development references stay outside the player-facing interface.
