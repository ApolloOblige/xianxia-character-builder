# Heritage rules

Each heritage archetype grants exactly +1 to one attribute while Beast / Plant Spirit Path is selected. Specific heritage names provide appearance and story flavor, with no additional mechanical bonus. Cultivation path itself grants no modifier. Origins continue to grant money and supplies only.

| Custom heritage | +1 Heart | Awakened Resolve |

The bonus is recalculated from the current archetype, never accumulated in the saved manual-modifier list. Switching archetypes replaces it; switching to another path deactivates it while retaining choices. Unknown base attributes remain blank. Body and Flow bonuses also contribute to Vitality and Qi when their other inputs are supplied. No global attribute cap is implemented. Old custom plant saves retain their names and use Plant Spirit.

## Design references

These attribute assignments are original house rules authorized by the owner, not published D&D or Monster of the Week rules.

- [D&D Free Rules: Creating a Character](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/creating-a-character) provides the reference for choices contributing to character statistics. Its ability-score scale and background increases are not imported into this five-attribute game.
- [Evil Hat: Monster of the Week hunter playbooks](https://evilhat.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Monster-of-the-Week-Hunter-Playbooks-Consolidated-2025.pdf) uses small ratings and +1 improvements. This informs the conservative single +1 allowance; the xianxia mapping is our own.
- [Wuxiaworld glossary](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/page/general-glossary-of-terms) supplies genre context for awakened plants and spiritual medicines such as ginseng and lingzhi.
- [The Metropolitan Museum of Art: Noble Virtues](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/noble-virtues) discusses Chinese plant symbolism, including bamboo resilience and plum courage.

The 50 plant selections include familiar plants and original fantasy variations such as Jade Bamboo and Cloudcap Mushroom. Fungus is grouped with plant spirits as a fantasy convenience. Names do not independently grant flight, armor, poison, healing, affinity or divine powers. Mythic names have the same one-point allowance as other archetypes.
