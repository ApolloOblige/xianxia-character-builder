# Disciplines, cultivation manuals and trades

The page follows Discipline → Cultivation Manuals → Trades & Talents → Weapons & Equipment. These are original playable adaptations made with the owner's authorization.

## Choices and effects

Choose one primary discipline, up to two distinct manuals, up to two distinct apprentice trades and one natural talent. The discipline selects a focus and supplies its specified starting weapon. It does not change Identity path, realm or permanent attributes. Two manuals describe training curricula rather than individual attack buttons. Manuals can be studied across disciplines; changing discipline retains learned manuals. The matching discipline manual is listed first.

Each selected manual, trade or talent supplies +1 only for its stated activity. Use at most one such training bonus on a roll, even when multiple selections overlap. Positive training, root, polarity, resonance and physique bonuses together are capped at +2 before drawbacks. No unlimited passive attribute increases or automatic learned-technique list are granted. Resource and core-attribute totals remain separate from these situational rules.

Training remains subject to realm and available equipment, materials, time and applicable roots. A manual does not supply a beast, Gu, rare flame or missing elemental root. The Poison-Eating Cauldron Method starts with sealed-vessel external refinement; the name does not confer toxin immunity. Supernatural weapon guidance depends on cultivation and practice, not an autonomous enchanted item.

## Context and sources

- [Xianxia Worldbuilding Setup](https://chatgpt.com/c/6a9b3b58-0160-83e9-88d7-bfd32ef9276d), especially the trades/weapon-focus list (fdc68b83-d0c6-45f6-8e92-50555bf18332), establishes the 15 crafting/support professions, weapon families and distinction between profession and combat style. Prior character discussions supplied the Poison-Eating Cauldron Method, Golden Thread Essence-Severing Art, Gale-Breath Furnace Art and Hundred Venom Discernment names. Descriptions and numbers here are new adaptations, not claims of quoted canon.
- [Wuxiaworld glossary](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/page/general-glossary-of-terms) supplies the genre context for cultivation methods, spiritual weapons, formations, alchemy and talismans. All paths continue to use the owner's shared realm ladder.
- [Splitting the Heavens, chapter 32](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/splitting-the-heavens/sth-chapter-32) illustrates a sword manual teaching control beyond brute strength. This informs curriculum descriptions rather than copying that novel's ranks or powers.
- [Battle Through the Heavens, chapter 6](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/battle-through-the-heavens/btth-chapter-6/) describes the importance of ingredients, flame and perception in alchemy. This supports prepared tools and controlled refinement; that novel's exact alchemist prerequisites are not imposed on this setting.
- [D&D Free Rules: Equipment](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/equipment) separates weapon training and artisan tools, informing the distinction between combat focus, profession and possessions. Its proficiency scale, damage tables and currency are not imported.

## Save compatibility

The five new selection fields default to empty in older saves. Existing discipline, technique, trade and equipment text remains intact. Duplicate or unknown structured manual/trade/talent selections are rejected on import. Selected conditional effects are compiled for reference without appending copies to the manual-modifier list. References remain outside the player interface.
